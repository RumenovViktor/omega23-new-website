The short version

What stays on your phone

The app stores the following on your device only. We can't see it, and it isn't sent to us or to our providers:

  • Your quiz answers, plans and daily limit
  • Your time on the apps you chose, your streak and your estimated time saved
  • Your unlock history and the reasons you chose
  • Your block-screen message

Unscroll uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework. When you pick apps to block, Apple gives the app private tokens instead of app names, so we can't see which apps you chose. The app gets your usage only as on-device signals that a time threshold has been reached.

Data from Apple's Screen Time framework is used only to provide the app's features to you. We don't share it, use it for advertising or advertising measurement, or give it to data brokers.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you use Delete my data or delete the app.
  • Analytics: about 1 year. Our provider deletes expired events in batches, so some may remain a little longer.
  • Subscription records: for as long as needed to provide your subscription, and afterwards as required for accounting, tax and legal reasons.

Your choices and rights

To erase everything stored on your phone, open Me → Delete my data in the app. This also lifts all blocks, clears the app's pending notifications and resets your anonymous analytics ID. Deleting your data doesn't cancel your subscription. To do that, go to your Apple account's subscription settings.

Delete my data can't erase analytics events or purchase records already sent to our providers. These aren't tied to your name, so we may need help identifying them. Contact us and we'll do what we can.

If you're in the EEA or UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Bulgaria, that's the Commission for Personal Data Protection. To exercise a right, email us. Other privacy laws may give you similar rights wherever you live.

Permissions

  • Screen Time: needed to block the apps you choose. You can turn it off in iPhone Settings, and blocks stop.
  • Notifications: used for unlock requests, reminders and summaries. These are created on your phone, not sent from our servers.

Children

Unscroll is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has used the app in a way that gave us their data, contact us.
