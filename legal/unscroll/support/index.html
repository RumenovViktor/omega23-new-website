A block won't lift

You can always get back to your own apps. Try these in order:

  • Open Unscroll, which rechecks your blocks every time it opens.
  • Go to Home and tap Unlock for a short unlock. Unlocks aren't available during a strict session.
  • Go to Me → Delete my data. This lifts every block straight away, even during a strict session, but it also erases your plans and stats.
  • Go to Settings → Screen Time on your iPhone and turn off Unscroll's access.

An app isn't being blocked

  • Open Me → Blocked apps and check that the app is on your list, and that Screen Time access is on.
  • Check that the plan is switched on and that its time has started.
  • Check that your trial or subscription is active. Blocks only run while it is.

Tapping Unlock on the block screen does nothing

The block screen can't open Unscroll directly, so it sends you a notification instead. Turn on notifications in Settings → Notifications → Unscroll, or open Unscroll and tap Unlock on Home.

Cancel your subscription or trial

In Unscroll, go to Me → Manage subscription. You can also go to Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

To avoid being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or current period ends. Deleting the app doesn't cancel your subscription.

Once your access ends, Unscroll lifts your blocks the next time you open it. Your plans stay saved in case you come back.

Get a refund

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore a purchase

On a new phone or after reinstalling, tap Restore on the paywall, or go to Me → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

My numbers don't match Screen Time

Your usage in Unscroll is rounded down to the nearest 5 to 15 minutes, and time saved is an estimate. The streak on the block screen updates when you open the app, so it can be a little behind until then.

Delete your data

Go to Me → Delete my data. This erases everything Unscroll stores on your phone and lifts all blocks. It doesn't cancel your subscription. Our Privacy Policy covers the limited data held by our providers and how to ask us about it.

What you need

An iPhone running iOS 16.4 or later, with Screen Time access allowed for Unscroll.
