Social Cleanup

Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version: Social Cleanup analyzes the Instagram data export that you download and choose to import. That analysis happens entirely on your phone. Your follower and following lists are never uploaded to us. We don't ask for your Instagram password, we don't log into Instagram, and we don't sell your data. On Android, the free plan shows ads from Google (see section 1.6). The iOS App has no ads.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Social Cleanup app for iOS and Android (the "App") handles, where it is stored, and what choices you have. The App is provided by Omega23 ("we", "us"). By using the App you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Use.

Social Cleanup is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc.

1. Information the App processes

1.1 Your Instagram data export

To use the App, you request a copy of your data from Instagram ("Download your information") and then choose that file in the App. From the export, the App reads only the followers and following files, specifically:

  • usernames of the accounts that follow you and that you follow;
  • links to those profiles;
  • the dates on which each follow happened, where Instagram includes them.

Every other part of the export, such as photos, videos, messages, comments, likes, and your personal information, is skipped and never read. The temporary copy of the export file is deleted once the import finishes.

1.2 Information you create in the App

The App stores the snapshots you import (the lists described above plus the date of each import) and your cleanup progress, such as which accounts you marked as done, skipped, or unavailable. It also stores your App settings.

1.3 Purchase information

If you buy Social Cleanup Pro, payment is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account (on iOS) or by Google through your Google account (on Android). We never see your payment card or billing details. To manage your purchase, our purchase provider RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and your purchase history (which product you bought, when, and whether it is active). This app user ID is stored on your device and is used only to manage your purchase. See section 3.

1.4 Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

Unless you turn them off, the App sends a small set of anonymous product events, such as "import completed" or "paywall viewed", to help us improve it. Each event includes the App version, operating system (iOS or Android) version and device type. The events are processed on our behalf by PostHog, Inc. (see section 3). These events:

  • never contain usernames, follower lists, file names, or file contents;
  • use rounded counts only (for example "1k–9k followers"), never exact numbers;
  • are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising or tracking;
  • do not rely on any identifier stored on your device. Each time you open the App counts as a new, separate visit;
  • are sent without your IP address being stored or used to work out your location;
  • can be turned off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage stats. When off, nothing is sent.

1.5 What we do not collect

  • Your Instagram password or login session. The App never asks for them.
  • Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.
  • On iOS: advertising identifiers. The iOS App has no ads and does not track you across other apps or websites.

1.6 Ads on Android (free plan only)

On Android, the free version of the App shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (in the EEA and UK, Google Ireland Limited). Social Cleanup Pro removes all ads. The iOS App has no ads. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google receives information directly from your device, including:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which Google may use to estimate your approximate location;
  • basic device and App information, such as device model, operating system version and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

Ads never receive your Instagram data: your follower lists, usernames and export stay on your device.

Where the law requires it, including in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. If you don't consent to personalized ads, you may see non-personalized ads or no ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings. Google's use of this information is governed by its own privacy policy; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

2. Where your data is processed and stored

Unzipping, reading and comparing your export all happen on your device. Your snapshots and cleanup progress are stored in a local database on your phone. We do not operate a server that receives your follower or following data, and we have no copy of it.

Because this data lives only on your device, we cannot recover it if you delete the App or lose your device. Depending on your device settings, it may be included in your device's backups, such as iCloud or computer backups on iOS or Google backups on Android, which are controlled by Apple or Google and by you.

4. What the App never does

  • Ask for your Instagram password or log into Instagram on your behalf.
  • Scrape Instagram or use unofficial Instagram APIs.
  • Follow or unfollow accounts automatically. Every unfollow is something you do yourself in Instagram.
  • Sell your data.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can:

  • delete individual snapshots in the History tab;
  • delete everything in Settings → Delete All Data;
  • delete the App, which removes all of its data from your device.

Purchase records held by Apple, Google and RevenueCat are kept as long as needed to provide your purchase and to meet legal and accounting obligations. Data that Google AdMob receives is kept under Google's own retention policies. To ask for your RevenueCat purchase record to be deleted, contact us at the address below.

Usage statistics are kept by PostHog for no longer than 7 years and then deleted. Because they are not linked to you or your device, we cannot find or delete the events of a particular person.

6. Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security, including its encryption and your screen lock. Communication with RevenueCat, PostHog, Apple and Google uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but keeping your data on your device greatly limits exposure.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU/EEA, the UK, and California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because your follower data never leaves your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. For data held by our service providers, email us at contact.omega23@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Where the GDPR applies, we process purchase data to perform our contract with you (providing the Pro access you bought), and optional usage statistics based on our legitimate interest in improving the App, which you can switch off at any time. On Android, personalized ads are shown only with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country) and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Effective" date above and, for material changes, let you know in the App. Continuing to use the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
