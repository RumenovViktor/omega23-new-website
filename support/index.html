Frequently asked questions

How do I get my Instagram data export?

In Instagram, open Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information and request a copy of your data that includes your followers and following. Instagram will notify you when the file is ready. Download it, then choose it in Social Cleanup.

Do you need my Instagram password?

No. Social Cleanup never asks for your password and never logs into Instagram. It only reads the export file you choose, and all analysis happens on your iPhone.

Why do my numbers differ from what Instagram shows?

Results are based on your export as of the date Instagram created it. Exports can include deactivated, deleted, or renamed accounts and may omit some information. "Lost followers" between snapshots can also be caused by accounts being deactivated or renamed, not only by unfollows.

Does the App unfollow accounts for me?

No. The App helps you review accounts one by one, but every unfollow is something you do yourself in Instagram.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

On your new or reinstalled device, open the App's Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, choose Social Cleanup, and cancel. Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Your data is stored only on your device. Delete individual snapshots in the History tab, delete everything in Settings → Delete All Data, or delete the App to remove all of its data.
